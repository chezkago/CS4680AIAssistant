# StitchWorth

StitchWorth is an AI-powered crochet pricing assistant for people who
sell handmade amigurumi animals.

## Problem

Crochet sellers often guess how much to charge for handmade products.
This can cause them to underprice their work, lose money, or charge
prices that do not reflect the time and materials required.

StitchWorth helps sellers calculate a cost-based price and receive an
AI-generated pricing recommendation.

## Features

- Calculates labor cost from hours, minutes, and hourly wage
- Calculates material and total production costs
- Allows users to include or exclude a profit percentage
- Compares the calculated price with three competitor prices
- Allows users to show or hide the pricing formula
- Generates an AI-recommended price range
- Generates a short product description
- Provides one practical business suggestion
- Uses a pink, feminine, and professional user interface
